About us

We sell the things we already use

Every product on this site started as something built for a paying client, then cleaned up enough to hand to a stranger.

How this started

We spent two years doing AI implementation work for small teams — the unglamorous kind, where the deliverable is a spreadsheet that does something useful on Monday morning. The same patterns kept coming up. The same prompts kept getting rewritten from scratch. The same three automations kept getting rebuilt.

So we packaged them. The first prompt pack went out to eleven people on a mailing list. It is now the best-selling product on this site.

What we will not do

There is a lot of noise in this market and most of it comes from the same three shortcuts. We avoid them:

  • No scraped prompt lists. If it has not been run on real work at least fifty times, it does not go in.
  • No fake urgency. No countdown timers that reset when you reload, no "only 3 left" on a digital file.
  • No subscription trap. You pay once. Updates are free forever. There is nothing to cancel.
  • No refund friction. Fourteen days, one email, refunded the same day. We do not ask why.

How products get updated

Models change and prompts rot. Every product gets reviewed each quarter against the current major assistants, and anything that has degraded gets rewritten. You get the new version by email at no cost — that is what "free updates for life" actually means here.

Who's behind it

A small team, split between building the products and answering support. Support is not outsourced and it is not a bot: when you email, one of the people who wrote the product writes back, usually within a day.
