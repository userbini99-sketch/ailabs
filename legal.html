Terms of sale

By buying from this store you agree to these terms. We sell digital products delivered by download. There is no physical shipment.

  • Delivery. Download links are shown on the confirmation screen immediately after payment and emailed to the address you provide. Links do not expire.
  • Pricing. All prices are in and exclude any tax that applies in your country. Applicable tax is calculated at checkout.
  • Availability. Products may be revised, renamed or retired. If you have already bought a product, you keep access to it and to its updates.
  • Accounts. Purchases are tied to the email address you check out with. Keep it accurate — update emails go there.
  • Acceptable use. You may not resell, sublicense or redistribute the files. See the licence section below.

Privacy

We collect the minimum needed to sell you a file and support you afterwards.

  • What we collect. Your name, email address, country (for tax), and what you bought.
  • Payment details. Card numbers go directly to our payment processor. They never touch our servers and we cannot see them.
  • What we do with it. Deliver your files, send update notices, and answer support. If you subscribe to the newsletter we email you weekly until you unsubscribe.
  • Who we share it with. The payment processor, the email provider, and nobody else. We do not sell customer data.
  • Your rights. Email us to get a copy of your data or have it deleted. We action requests within thirty days.
  • Cookies. This site stores your cart, your theme preference and any applied discount code in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a third party from those.

Refunds

Fourteen days, no questions asked. Email us within fourteen days of purchase and we refund in full, the same working day.

  • You do not need to justify the request or delete what you downloaded.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method and typically land within five business days, depending on your bank.
  • After a refund, access to future updates for that product ends.
  • We reserve the right to decline repeat refunds from the same customer on the same product.

Product licence

What you may and may not do with the files you buy.

You may

  • Use the products for your own work, personal or commercial.
  • Use them while delivering paid work to your own clients (prompt packs, templates and automations).
  • Modify and adapt them for your own use.
  • Use outputs generated with them commercially, with no attribution required.

You may not

  • Resell, relicense or give away the files themselves, modified or not.
  • Publish them anywhere publicly accessible, including free download sites and shared drives.
  • Include them in another product you sell.
  • Share course access beyond the seats you bought. Courses are single-seat unless stated otherwise.
